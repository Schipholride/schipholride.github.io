Fixed-price taxi to and from Schiphol Airport — how it works

SchipholRide is a Dutch airport-transfer service covering Amsterdam, Haarlem, Leiden, The Hague, Rotterdam, Utrecht and the wider Randstad. You see your total fare before you book — no meter, no night surcharge, no surprises when you land.

  • Fixed total price upfront — the fare you see in the booking widget is the fare you pay.
  • Live flight tracking — if your flight is delayed, your pickup time shifts automatically, free.
  • 60 minutes of free waiting time after landing, day and night.
  • Available 24/7 — the 3 a.m. price equals the 2 p.m. price.
  • Luggage included — one suitcase plus one carry-on per person.
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.
  • Pay online — iDEAL, credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

Booking takes 30 seconds

  1. Enter your pickup address and destination at schipholride.nl/en — your fixed fare appears instantly.
  2. Choose a vehicle class (Comfort, Premium, Van for up to 8, or VIP) and pay securely online.
  3. Your driver tracks your flight and waits for you in the arrivals hall — meet & greet included.

FAQ

Do you serve arrivals at night?
Yes — 24/7. Your driver monitors the flight and waits up to 60 minutes free after landing.
Which areas do you cover?
The whole Randstad and beyond. The full city list with fixed fares is at schipholride.nl/en.
Can I book for a group?
Yes — the Van class seats up to 8 passengers with luggage.
Child seats?
Available on request — just note it while booking.
